The Game Begins - Short Film

A comedy-thriller short film about friendship, competition, and chaos… all starting with a simple game night. Seven friends think they’re just playing a game—until the game begins to play them.

About the Film

Seven friends gather for a routine game night, but when a mysterious video starts on the TV, the night turns into a competitive, mysterious, and hilarious chase across the church.

Clues, teamwork, and playful rivalry collide as they navigate cryptic messages, dark hallways, and their own over-the-top personalities. “The Game Begins” is a story about strategy, friendship, and laughter—sometimes nervous, sometimes loud, and always unpredictable.
