Final Internship Reflection

A semester of communication strategy, media development, and hands-on ministry experience.

Overview

Over the course of this internship with facCOMMS, I was able to gain hands-on experience in church communications, media production, and team organization. What began as a learning opportunity quickly turned into an active role in planning, creating, and managing communication efforts for a variety of events and ministries.

Throughout the semester, I contributed to both the creative and strategic sides of communication, helping to ensure that messaging remained clear, consistent, and engaging across platforms.

Quick Summary

Main focus: Communication strategy, social media promotion, Media Team development, and real-world ministry experience.

Key Responsibilities & Projects

During this internship, I was involved in a wide range of responsibilities that contributed to the overall communication strategy of the church.

  • Planned and created social media promotions for weekly services and special events
  • Developed consistent messaging and visual style across platforms
  • Contributed to facWEEKLY email content and communication flow
  • Assisted in website updates and content organization
  • Helped plan and execute the Media Team Orientation
  • Organized and structured Media Team scheduling systems using Planning Center
  • Participated in facCOMMS meetings to plan future promotions and communication strategy

These responsibilities allowed me to move beyond observation and into active participation, where I was able to contribute ideas and see them implemented in real time.

Media Team Development

One of the most significant projects during this internship was helping develop and organize the Media Team. This included contributing to the planning of the Media Team Orientation, thinking through team structure, and helping implement scheduling systems.

This experience gave me insight into leadership, organization, and the importance of building systems that support long-term sustainability rather than short-term solutions.

Growth & Learning

This internship helped me grow both creatively and professionally. I developed a better understanding of how communication works in a real-world setting, especially within a ministry context where clarity, tone, and timing are all critical.

I also learned the importance of adaptability. Plans often changed, timelines shifted, and priorities evolved, which required flexibility and problem-solving. Being part of that process helped me become more confident in navigating real-world work environments.

Key Takeaway

Strong communication is not just about creativity—it requires structure, consistency, and adaptability.

Challenges

One of the main challenges during this internship was working through transitions within the communications team. Changes in leadership required adjustments in workflow and responsibility, which highlighted the importance of having clear systems and communication processes in place.

Additionally, balancing multiple projects at once required strong time management and prioritization, especially when handling promotions with overlapping timelines.

Reflection

Overall, this internship was a valuable and formative experience. It allowed me to apply what I’ve learned in my coursework to a real-world setting while also developing new skills in communication, media, and teamwork.

It also gave me a clearer direction for my future, particularly in pursuing work related to media, communications, and creative production. The experience reinforced my interest in this field and helped me better understand how my skills can be applied in a professional environment.

Looking Ahead

Moving forward, I plan to continue building on the skills I developed during this internship, particularly in photography, videography, and media production. I am also interested in expanding my experience in communication strategy and content creation.

This internship provided a strong foundation that I can continue to build on as I pursue future opportunities in the creative and media field.
