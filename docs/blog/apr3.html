Media Team Structure + Safety Discussion

A significant part of this week’s conversation focused on how to structure the media team moving forward, particularly regarding recruitment and safety. This included discussing policies around minors serving on the team and how to responsibly manage church equipment.

One idea I contributed was the possibility of a “buddy system,” where younger volunteers could still be involved while paired with a cleared adult. This would allow them to learn and gain experience without being solely responsible for equipment, while also maintaining safety and accountability.

  • Discussion of age requirements for equipment use
  • Safety and clearance considerations for volunteers
  • Potential recruitment of new team members
  • Exploration of structured systems for training younger volunteers
