Media Team Orientation Planning

A major part of this week’s discussion centered on building a meaningful orientation for media volunteers. The goal is not only to introduce people to the ministry, but also to create a strong foundation of technical skill, church-specific expectations, and a healthy view of creativity as an act of worship.

Tentative Orientation Topics

  • Introductions
  • Vision and goals of the media ministry
  • Photography training at a base level for all volunteers
  • Rule of Thirds and how to capture strong shots
  • Camera settings for Sony and Canon cameras
  • Photography and creativity as worship
  • Where to stand during services and events
  • What to capture — and what not to capture
  • What volunteers should wear
  • Examples of photos and media our team has taken
  • Volunteer expectations and code of conduct
  • Church camera use, including straps and check-in/check-out procedures

We also talked about making this orientation a combination of helpful training videos and our own commentary based on experience, church culture, and familiarity with the FAC facility layout.
