“What’s Up” Communication Format

We discussed refining the “What’s Up” post format so it can serve as a recognizable communication piece for upcoming events. For now, it functions similarly to a monthly calendar-style post with language such as “Join us in March.” However, the long-term goal is to shift toward carousel posts where each slide highlights a specific ministry or announcement.

For inspiration, we looked at the Instagram account for Church of the City New York (@church.nyc), which uses a carousel approach where each slide contains a different announcement. This format could make church communication clearer and easier to share on social media.

Easter Preparation

Easter preparation continues to move forward. Pastor Ben approved the Easter graphic and stage design concepts. As part of the media preparation for the service, I am currently creating a lyric and visual intro video for the song “All Hail King Jesus.” The goal of the video is to visually introduce the Easter story and transition into worship using cinematic visuals and lyrics.

The video will be created primarily using motion graphics and visual elements in After Effects before being finalized in Premiere Pro. This project is an opportunity to combine storytelling, worship themes, and visual design to create a meaningful opening moment for the Easter service.
