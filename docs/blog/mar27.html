Child Dedication Messaging

A key piece of communication this week centered on preparing messaging for the upcoming Child Dedication service. This included focusing on the idea of “widening the circle,” emphasizing the role of both family and church community in a child’s spiritual growth.

The messaging highlights that Child Dedication is not about committing a child to the church long-term or replacing baptism, but rather about a parent’s commitment to raise their child in faith while inviting the church to walk alongside them. This concept helps clearly communicate both the purpose and the heart behind the event.

Key Idea

“Widening the circle” reinforces that faith formation is not meant to happen in isolation, but within a supportive community of believers.
