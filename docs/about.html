About Me

Digital Media Graduate | Communications Coordinator | Video Editor | Photographer

I recently graduated from Penn State Behrend with a Bachelor of Arts in Digital Media, Arts, and Technology, along with minors in Communication Arts and Mass Media and Media Production. My passion lies in visual storytelling through video production, photography, graphic design, and multimedia content that blends creativity with technical skill.

I currently serve as the Communications Coordinator at First Alliance Church, where I manage visual communications and digital media across the organization. My work includes photography, videography, video editing, graphic design, social media content, website updates, branding, and promotional campaigns for events and ministries. This role has allowed me to develop strong storytelling, marketing, and content strategy skills while collaborating with staff and volunteers to effectively communicate the church’s mission.

Alongside my professional work, I am starting my own media and photography business, offering photography, videography, and creative media services for individuals, organizations, and events. I have also gained hands-on experience through volunteer work with The Band 814 and First Alliance Church, where I created photography, videography, and social media content for events and promotions. These experiences have strengthened my creative problem-solving abilities as well as my technical proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator, and InDesign.

Whether I'm producing a documentary, capturing an event, designing a marketing campaign, or creating content for social media, I strive to combine creativity with technical precision. My goal is to create meaningful visual experiences that engage audiences, tell compelling stories, and leave a lasting impact.
